Mara stayed late in the accounting office to finish payroll before the audit.
She kept glancing at the wall clock above the door, which showed half past seven.
She matched each total against paper receipts, initialed each page, and stacked them for scanning.
At what she took for eight, she sent the files and shut down her computer.
Tired, she reached for her phone and saw the screen read nearly ten.
The wall clock had stopped during the afternoon without her noticing.
She gathered her bag, turned off the lights, and left the office determined to check the time twice.
