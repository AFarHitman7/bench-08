on the page

Equations and tables

Multi-line derivations, aligned environments, matrices and ruled tables come out with their structure kept, not flattened to text.

02

Revision history per page

Each conversion is stored against its photo. Compare any two revisions line by line when you change a symbol or a bracket.

Export to .tex or .pdf
